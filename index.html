Who could read?

I'm Ada, and I study history. This page asks how one machine changed the answer.

Copying gave way to printing

Before the press, each book was copied by hand, and most Europeans could not read one. Around 1450 in Mainz, Johannes Gutenberg put movable metal type to work in a press, and the same page could be printed again and again.

Print did not teach anyone to read. It gave more people something to read, and over the following centuries that changed who could take part in an argument. Access to knowledge shapes who gets a voice.
